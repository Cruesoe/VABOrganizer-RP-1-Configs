**Sorts RP-1's huge parts list into VAB Organizer subcategories and labels every engine with the country that built it.**

RP-1 adds hundreds of parts, and finding the right engine or tank in the editor takes a while. I wrote these configs while playing RP-1 so that everything sits where you'd look for it.

## Features

- Engines are grouped the way you plan a rocket: Ground Lit, Single Ignition, Multi Ignition, ORSC, FRSC, Hydrolox, and Thrusters & Small Engines.
- Solid motors are split by vacuum thrust, from Tiny Solids under 10 kN to Extra Large Solids over 1000 kN.
- Engines carry a country label (USA, USSR, Europe, Asia or Generic), so you can build a historically accurate rocket.
- RCS thrusters are sorted by thrust size, and scalable RCS blocks stay in the general RCS group.
- Fuel tanks are split into Conventional, Isogrid, Balloon, Service Module and Shielded tanks.
- Antennas are split into Omnidirectional and Directional, and experiments into Sample and Transmittable.
- Fairings, interstages, boattails, launch clamps, decals, fuel valves and procedural wings each get their own group.
- Modular Launch Pads parts are sorted into stands, plates, rails, bases, towers, swing arms, hold downs, and the Soyuz, Saturn V and Shuttle launch sets.
- HullCam VDS cameras and Smart Parts get their own groups when those mods are installed.
- English and Russian text.

## Requirements

- [RP-1 Express Install](https://github.com/KSP-RO/RP-1/wiki/RP-1-Express-Install)
- [VAB Organizer](https://github.com/post-kerbin-mining-corporation/VABOrganizer)
- [Module Manager](https://github.com/sarbian/ModuleManager)

## Compatibility

Made for the default RP-1 Express Install. Modular Launch Pads, HullCam VDS Continued and Smart Parts support only applies when they are installed.
Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.
